Albuquerque Marathon & Half Marathon ▼

📍 Runners through neighborhood · 7:30 AM – 1:30 PM
Community

On Saturday, April 11, 2026, RunFit will be hosting the Inaugural Albuquerque Marathon along with the 22nd Annual Albuquerque Half Marathon, as well as a 10K, 5K, and Kids K.

⚠️ No Road Closures — All streets will remain open to vehicle traffic. However, you may notice an increased presence of runners, volunteers, and event staff. Please use caution when driving.
📍 Start/Finish Location

Sagebrush Community Church
6440 Coors Blvd NW
Albuquerque, NM 87120

🏃 Start Times
  • Marathon: 7:30 AM
  • Half Marathon & Relay: 8:30 AM
  • 10K: 9:00 AM
  • 5K: 9:15 AM
  • Kids K: 10:15 AM

Runners will be on course from approximately 7:30 AM – 1:30 PM.

🗺️ Route Through Our Neighborhood

South of Montano Blvd., the Marathon route follows the pedestrian/bike path east of the Rio Grande, then continues along Tingley Drive, Mountain Rd., Candelaria Blvd., and north on Rio Grande Blvd. to Montano Blvd.

Annual Neighborhood Meeting ▼

📍 Albuquerque Country Club, Banquet Room · 6:00 PM
Annual

All residents of the Huning Castle neighborhood are cordially invited and encouraged to attend the 2026 Annual Neighborhood Meeting. All residents of Huning Castle are automatically enrolled as members of the Association.

💵 Membership Contributions — Dues are not required, but suggested contributions of $25 (individual residents) and $40 (business members) are welcome and encouraged. Check or cash ONLY at the meeting, or pay via Zelle through your bank.
📋 Schedule
  • Registration opens at 6:00 PM
  • Meeting commences promptly at 6:30 PM
📝 Agenda
  • 1. Call to order & opening remarks — Brenda Marks
  • 2. Adoption of minutes from April 8, 2026 regular board meeting
  • 3. Special Guest: Carl Christensen, Bernalillo County Water Authority — resources, rebates & programs to save on your water bill
  • 4. Open floor — ten minutes for residents to speak
  • 5. APD Crime/Safety Report — Brittany Martinez, Crime Prevention Specialist
  • 6. HCN Safety Patrol Report — Rudy Garcia
  • 7. Committee reports: Quorum · Treasury (Kendall Corley) · HCALERT (Lara Bryson) · Website & Communications
  • 8. Recognition of departing board members: Erika Harding, Anna Lamberson, Lynn Hightower
  • 9. Announcement of new board members (2026–2029)
  • 10. Adjournment
