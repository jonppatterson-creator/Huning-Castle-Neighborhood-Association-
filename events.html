Backyard Story Series: Migration ▼

📍 Historic Aldo Leopold House · 4:00 – 6:00 PM
Community

The Leopold Writing Program presents its November Backyard Story Series, an evening of storytelling at the Historic Aldo Leopold House, right here in our neighborhood. All are welcome to come listen or share a story.

🕊️ This Month's Theme: Migration — Stories of migration, movement across borders, journeys down rivers, and travels through the land, whether your own or that of the more-than-human world.
💭 Questions to Inspire Your Story
  • What has migration taught you about your connection to land?
  • Have you been changed by the migration of your kin, or by shifts in migratory pathways?
  • What calls us to move, and what makes us stay?
